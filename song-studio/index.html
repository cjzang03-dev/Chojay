Song structure

Your song plays these parts in order. Tap a part to start playback or recording from it. Turn on Loop to repeat just that part while you write.

Rhymes

Drums

One bar, 16 steps, repeated. Tap squares to add or remove hits. Each group of four is one beat. Give a part its own beat to make a verse lighter or a chorus hit harder.

Instrument channels

Each channel is one instrument with its own notes. Choose a channel to edit it in the piano roll.

Chords & bass

Piano roll

Tap an empty square to add a note, drag a note to move it, drag its right edge to make it longer, and tap a note to remove it. Tinted rows are in your key; amber rows are the notes of the chord in that bar.

Playlist

Your whole song on a timeline. Tap the ruler to choose where playback and recording start. Tap a part to select it. Drag a vocal clip left or right to line it up.

Add your singing

  1. Press Play and record yourself singing along with your phone's voice memo app.
  2. Add the recording below. It lands at the start of the selected part, or the start of the song.
  3. Line it up with the beat buttons on the take, then try Autotune, harmonies or Double.

Microphone

  1. Put on headphones so the music doesn't leak into your vocal.
  2. Turn on the microphone and sing a little. The meter should move into the yellow, not the red.
  3. Pick a part on the Song tab (or none to start from the top), then press Rec. You get one bar of clicks first.

Takes

Every take plays along with the song, so you can stack harmonies and doubles. Mute the ones you don't want.
